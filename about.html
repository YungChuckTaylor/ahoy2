Since 2018

A fellowship, not a spectacle.

The Association of Humble and Obedient Youths exists so that young people in Nigeria, Egypt, Canada and the United Kingdom can be useful — to their street, their craft and their word — without first becoming a brand.

The seal

Why a wheel, a sail, a red disc.

The wheel is stewardship: someone must hold the heading. The sail is youth — canvas that only works when it accepts the wind. The red disc is the small, serious centre we try not to abandon: humility, obedience to duty, and the refusal to spend other people’s money in the dark.

“We would rather be accurate than impressive.”

HUMBLE

Humility

We count a youth once. We do not rent stages. Photographs prefer hands at work to faces under lights.

OBEY

Obedience

To the register, the cost sheet, the visit checklist. Obedience here means keeping the rule when nobody is clapping.

YOUTH

Youth

The Primer is revised by members under 25. Chapters are steered by people who still sit in the circles they run.

How a chapter works

A desk, a register, a visit.

The desk

Each country keeps a lean steward desk — one or two people who pay invoices, file papers and answer letters. They do not vote on their own projects.

The register

Names, kits, stipends and visits are written down. The public archive is the register with the private bits removed.

The visit

A verification desk that did not spend the money turns up, often unannounced, and compares the site to the completion report.

What we will not do

A short list of refusals.

We will not pay sitting allowances. We will not inflate the headline count. We will not publish a child’s face without a marked consent card, and even then we prefer not to. We will not take a grant that forbids us from publishing the cost sheet.

If a partner needs a different kind of organisation, we say so early and remain friends.
