Annual Report 2023

The fifth year of the Association. Canada’s chapter completed its first full winter camp. Egypt opened the Alexandria sewing-and-digital cohort. Nigeria retired the last of the 2019 sitting-allowance habit in practice, not only on paper.

The pamphlet How we count a youth served was adopted by the four desks in May. The Toronto Public Library study-hall partnership began in September. No sitting allowances were paid in any chapter.
