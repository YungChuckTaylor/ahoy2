How we work

Hands on the wheel. Hearts in the work.

AHOY was formed by youths who were tired of being photographed on rented stages. We count a person once. We do not pay sitting allowances. Field visits are made by a desk that does not run the project.

The ship's wheel on our seal is not decoration. It is a reminder that someone must steer, and that steering is a duty, not a performance.
